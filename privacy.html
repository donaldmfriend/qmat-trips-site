Privacy Policy for QMAT Trips

Effective Date: November 26, 2026

QMAT Trips (com.qmat.trips) is an Android application built for private friend-group trip planning and expense tracking.

Information We Access

When you sign in with Google, QMAT Trips requests access strictly to:

  • Basic Profile (openid, email, profile): Used to display your signed-in Google account inside the app and identify who logged an expense or photo.
  • Google Sheets (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets): Used solely to read and write the shared QMAT Trips Google Sheet that you create or connect by URL.
  • Google Drive (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file): Used solely to create and access the QMAT Trips master spreadsheet and trip photo files created or selected by the app.

Data Storage & Sharing

QMAT Trips does not run any external backend server or analytics tracker. All trip data, expense rows, itineraries, and photos remain directly in your Google account's shared Google Sheet and Google Drive folders. We do not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

Contact

For questions or data removal requests, contact donaldmfriend@gmail.com.
