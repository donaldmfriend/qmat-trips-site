QMAT Trips

QMAT Trips is a private group travel companion for managing shared trip expenses, multi-currency settlements, daily itineraries, and photo-only shared trip albums using a user-selected Google Sheet and Google Drive folder.

  • Shared Expenses & Settlements: Equal and ad-hoc splits backed by Google Sheets formulas.
  • Daily Agenda: Shared group plans and per-person itinerary drafts.
  • Shared Trip Photos: Photos-only album stored in the trip's Google Drive folder.
