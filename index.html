How to Install QMAT Trips on iPhone / iPad

Apple doesn't allow direct `.ipa` downloads from websites, so our Apple version installs straight from Safari onto your Home Screen with the QMAT Trips icon (free, never expires, shares the exact same trips & expenses with Android):

1
Tap Open QMAT Trips iPhone App below in Safari (if you opened this link inside Messages, Instagram, or WhatsApp, tap the Safari compass icon first to open it in Safari).
2
In Safari, tap the Share ↑ button at the bottom of your screen (or top-right on iPad).
3
Scroll down and tap + Add to Home Screen, then tap Add.
4
Open QMAT Trips from your Home Screen, tap Sign in with Google, and paste Donald's shared Google Sheet link.

Quick Setup After Installing

  1. Open QMAT Trips and tap Sign in with Google. If Google shows an "unverified app" screen, tap Advanced → Go to QMAT Trips (unsafe) → Continue.
  2. Paste Donald's shared QMAT Trips Master Google Sheet link and tap Join Shared QMAT Sheet.
